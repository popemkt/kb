3 · A query that does not lift. Lifting is all-or-nothing: a hand-written clause outside the vocabulary, or a construct outside the IR, keeps the query in the EDN tab. The Conditions tab says which.

Likewise for a query outside the IR (:with), or text that does not parse (with its line and column).
